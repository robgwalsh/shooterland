## GeneralUser GS sound bank

`assets/music/GeneralUserGS.sf3` is GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins, in the SF3 (Ogg Vorbis) build published by the SpessaSynth project. Its licence (`assets/music/GeneralUserGS-LICENSE.txt`) permits use in software projects and asks that copies be hosted rather than hot-linked, which this package does. Updates and the original bank are available from the author's website, schristiancollins.com.

`assets/music/synth-worker.js` bundles spessasynth_lib and spessasynth_core (Apache License 2.0, Copyright spessasus). The main-thread half is a runtime dependency of this package.
